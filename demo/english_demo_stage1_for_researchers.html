More about this page and how the list was made
Purpose: first-pass filtering. Mark which candidate expressions are worth putting to the panel. Data: Public demonstration built from the corona benchmark, showing open-licensed material only: published Metaphor Menu entries and the CC-licensed #ReframeCovid collection, attributed to their contributors. Reddit text is not redistributed. Your judgements are compared with the software's ordering afterwards, which is how we learn whether that ordering can be trusted — so the ordering is deliberately hidden from you.

160 expressions in random order (identical phrasings are shown once with a count; 4 further passages are folded under them, and one label covers all of them). Nothing about how the software rated or ranked them is shown, so your judgement is your own. Mark the ones worth putting to the panel. Some of these were rated poorly by the software and some highly — which is deliberate, and comparing your choices with its order afterwards is how we find out whether the ordering can be trusted.

  1. 600 quality-filtered public posts from two COVID illness communities on Reddit (March 2020 - July 2021), split into 1,823 passages, with items of known status hidden among them: 17 published Metaphor Menu entries and 130 CC-licensed #ReframeCovid entries as known-good, and 100 figurative disease mentions about other topics as known-bad probes.
  2. A local model family proposed metaphor candidates from every passage. A second model family independently checked whether each proposed phrase was metaphorical in its passage; 1,665 verified candidates went on to screening.
  3. The workflow then recorded separate outputs for metaphor validity and illness relevance, lived experience, and resemblance to published Metaphor Menu entries (score 0-10).
  4. Overlapping spans of the same expression were merged, and the four planted entries quoted inside the scoring prompt were removed: 1,490 ranked candidates, of which this demo shows the 415 with open-licensed text (Reddit text is not redistributed).
  5. This page: the 120 highest-placed candidates in the system's ordering, plus 40 drawn unseen from deeper down the list, shuffled together — 160 rows in all (identical phrasings merged). Nothing is discarded: the other candidates remain in the ranked list and in the project team's working copy.
Before you start — who is labelling?

Your labels are saved in this browser and never leave it until you press Export. The exported file contains only candidate numbers and your verdicts — no quoted text, so it is safe to email back for merging.
